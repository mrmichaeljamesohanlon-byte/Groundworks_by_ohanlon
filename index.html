How our quotes work

Clear prices. No surprises.

01 / LABOUR

Time × agreed rate

Estimates show hours and hourly rate separately. £20/hour is the example starting figure in this demo, not a fixed advertised rate for every job.

02 / MATERIALS & DISPOSAL

Costs shown separately

Stone, membrane, edging, skips and disposal are listed distinctly when applicable. Nothing is silently added to the total.

03 / FAIR ADJUSTMENTS

Scope agreed in advance

If work takes significantly less time, the final labour charge may be reduced as agreed. Unexpected work or extra drainage work is discussed before proceeding.

Estimates are indicative until site inspection and written agreement. Drainage pipe connections or specialist work beyond excavation may require coordination with another contractor. VAT status, materials, waste and access conditions are confirmed in the final quotation. This calculator is not an invoice or a binding offer.
